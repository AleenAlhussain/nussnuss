NussNuss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

NussNuss ("the app") helps you split shared expenses. This policy explains what happens to your information. Short version: we don't collect it.

What stays on your device

Your name, groups, people, expenses and payments are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data, and there are no user accounts.

Sharing a link

When you choose to share a group, the app creates a link that contains that group's data inside the link itself (after the # sign). The part after # is not sent to our web server when the link is opened; it is read by the page in the browser. Anyone you send the link to can see that group's names, expenses and balances. Only share links with people you trust.

Purchases

If you buy NussNuss Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to check whether you own Plus. These companies process purchase information under their own privacy policies: Apple, Google, RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data, no contact list access.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a group in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
