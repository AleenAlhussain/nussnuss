# Seasonal campaigns, 2026–2027

People install a bill-splitting app at the moment they share costs. These are the moments, the message for each, and what to switch on in the app beforehand. Dates for Islamic holidays depend on the moon sighting; the ones below are the expected UAE dates ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/ramadan-eid-al-fitr-eid-al-adha-2027-likely-dates)).

| When | Moment | English line | Arabic line | In the app |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Now to mid-December | Winter trips and camping | One group for the whole trip. Free, no limits. | رحلة وحدة، حساب واحد. مجاني وبلا حدود. | Trip template, offline mode, Trip Pass |
| 25 Jan – 7 Feb 2027 | Before Ramadan | Iftars all month, one group. | إفطارات الشهر كله في مجموعة وحدة. | Iftar template; suggestions switch to إفطار / سحور |
| Ramadan, from about 8 Feb 2027 | Group iftars every night | Hold + to add tonight's iftar again. | اضغط مطولًا على + وكرّر إفطار الليلة. | Repeat last, weekly reminders |
| Last ten days of Ramadan | Eid gifts and clothes | Collect for Eid gifts without the chasing. | اجمعوا العيديات بدون ملاحقة. | Eid template, Remind, share card |
| Eid al-Fitr, about 9–10 Mar 2027 | Family outings | Who owes who, in one picture. | مين عليه لمين، في صورة وحدة. | Share summary card |
| Eid al-Adha, about 16–17 May 2027 | Shared sacrifice and family trips | Split it by shares, fairly. | قسّموها بالحصص، بالعدل. | Shares split, money circles |
| June – August 2027 | Summer travel | Any currency, the right rate. | أي عملة، بالسعر الصحيح. | Live rates (Plus), Trip Pass |
| September 2027 | University year and new flats | Rent, internet, groceries: set them once. | الإيجار والإنترنت والمقاضي: سجّلها مرة وحدة. | Flat template, recurring bills (Plus) |

Rules for every campaign: no competitor names, no invented reviews or testimonials, no "limited time" pressure on the free app. Show the real product (screens from the `web-screens` CI artifact or the phone apps).

**Weekly rhythm during Ramadan**: one short video a week showing a single feature (repeat last, remind, share card, money circle), posted before iftar; the share card itself is the best organic channel, since every card people post shows the NussNuss line.
