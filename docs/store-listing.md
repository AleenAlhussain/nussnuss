## English

**App name** (30): NussNuss: Split Bills
**Subtitle, App Store** (30): Free, no limits, no ads
**Short description, Google Play** (80): Split bills with friends for free. No daily limit, no ads. Arabic and English.

**Description**

Split it fair, without the awkward part.

NussNuss keeps track of shared costs with friends, family and flatmates and works out who owes whom with the fewest transfers. It is free with no limits: as many groups, people and expenses as you like, every day, with no ads.

• See where you stand the moment you open it: one overall balance, and the one thing worth doing next.
• Add an expense in three taps: the amount, a suggested title, save.
• Friends join from a short link or a QR code, in their browser. No app, no sign-up.
• Settle everything with one person across all your groups in one go.
• Activity shows who added what in every group, and your share of it.
• Faces, not just names: a photo, an emoji or an initial, and a drawn cover for every group.
• Any of 41 currencies with the right decimals, including three-decimal dinars.
• Split equally or unequally: drag a share onto someone, or split by amounts, percent or item from a receipt.
• Settle up in one tap: pay with the IBAN, InstaPay or PayPal link your friend shared, then mark it paid.
• Send a friendly reminder on WhatsApp, or share a "who owes who" card to the group.
• Works offline; everything syncs when you are back.
• Arabic and English, with true right-to-left layout and Arabic digits typed straight in.
• Money circles (جمعية): track turns, who paid and who is late.

NussNuss Plus (optional) saves you time: recurring bills that add themselves, weekly reminders sent by the app, live exchange rates, spending by category, CSV and print, and every look. A Trip Pass opens Plus for everyone in one group for 30 days.

NussNuss never holds or moves your money.

**Keywords, App Store** (100): split,bill,bills,expense,shared,friends,trip,travel,roommate,flatmate,iou,group,debt,owe,ramadan
**What's new**: A new Home with your overall balance and next step, people across groups with Settle everything, Activity, profile photos and drawn group covers, swipe and hold on expenses, drag to split, and invites by QR code.

## Privacy answers

**App Store privacy label** — Data linked to you: *Name* (the display name), *User content* (group names, expense titles and amounts; a profile photo if the person adds one), *Identifiers* (account id; Apple or Google sign-in id if linked), *Contact info* (email, only when Sign in with Apple or Google shares it), *Purchases* (store transaction ids). Used for: app functionality only. Data used to track you: **none**. Diagnostics: crash message and stack only, not linked to identity, when the server turns crash reports on. Usage data (*Product interaction*): how long five tasks take, not linked to identity and not used for tracking, only when the server turns task timings on.

**Google Play data safety** — Collected: personal info (name; email only with Google sign-in), financial info (purchase history; payment details the user chooses to share with friends, such as an IBAN), app activity (in-app content: groups and expenses), app info and performance (crash logs and task timings, optional, not linked to the person), photos (a profile photo, optional, shown to the person's groups). Shared with third parties: **no**. Encrypted in transit: **yes**. Users can request deletion: **yes**, in Settings (Delete my account), and download their data (Download my data). Not for children.

**Age rating**: no objectionable content; no gambling; no user-to-user chat (expense titles are visible only inside a group). 4+ / Everyone.
